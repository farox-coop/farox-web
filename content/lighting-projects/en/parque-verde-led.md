## Project Overview

Parque Verde is a major urban park that needed a complete outdoor lighting overhaul to improve nighttime safety while minimizing light pollution and energy costs.

## Challenge

The park's existing high-pressure sodium lamps were:

- Consuming excessive energy with no dimming capability
- Creating significant light pollution affecting nearby residential areas
- Difficult and expensive to maintain
- Providing poor color rendering that compromised safety

## Solution

We deployed a mesh-networked LED lighting system featuring:

- **Solar-powered LED bollards** along pedestrian pathways
- **Adaptive street lights** with motion sensors that brighten on detection and dim when idle
- **Tunable color temperature** that shifts from cool white during evening hours to warm amber at night
- **Central control platform** for remote monitoring, scheduling, and fault detection

## Results

- **78% reduction** in grid energy consumption through solar integration
- **Light pollution reduced** by 65% with precision optics
- **Zero maintenance outages** in the first 12 months
- Recognized by the city as a model for sustainable urban infrastructure
