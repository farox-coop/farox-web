GenIA brings together solutions to govern AI usage, organize internal knowledge, run models on your own infrastructure, and build agents tailored to real processes.

- **More control over AI** — Centralize access to different models, define permissions and policies, control costs, and protect sensitive information.
- **Knowledge under your control** — Organize information from different areas into a unified, up-to-date knowledge base that can be queried with AI.
- **Your own infrastructure and processes** — Run models on your own infrastructure and build intelligent agents tailored to your organization's real needs.
