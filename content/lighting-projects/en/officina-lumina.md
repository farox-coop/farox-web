## Project Overview

Officina Lumina is a 5,000 m² office complex that required a full lighting retrofit to reduce energy consumption and improve workplace comfort.

## Challenge

The existing fluorescent lighting system was outdated, consuming excessive energy and providing poor color rendering. The client needed a solution that would:

- Reduce energy consumption by at least 50%
- Improve light quality for employee wellbeing
- Enable smart scheduling and occupancy-based control
- Maintain compliance with local building codes

## Solution

We designed and implemented a DALI-based smart lighting system with:

- **LED fixtures** with tunable white color temperature (2700K–6500K)
- **Occupancy sensors** in every zone for automatic dimming
- **Daylight harvesting** sensors near windows to balance natural and artificial light
- **Central management dashboard** for real-time monitoring and scheduling

## Results

- **62% reduction** in energy consumption
- **Employee satisfaction** scores improved by 40%
- **Payback period** of 2.8 years
- Full integration with the building's BMS (Building Management System)
