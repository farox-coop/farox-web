## About

With SlotMatch, you can propose time slots, share a link, and find the best time for all participants.

- **Time Zone** — IANA zones, automatically detected for guests, stored in UTC.

- **Make a simple choice** — Drag across the grid using the touchscreen or mouse; the heat map shows the overlap of groups.

- **Decide when** — Find a time slot when everyone is free for the duration of your meeting.
