## Project Overview

Galería Artificial is a 1,200 m² contemporary art space requiring museum-grade lighting that adapts to changing exhibitions without manual recabling.

## Challenge

Art galleries have unique lighting demands:

- Different artworks require different color temperatures and beam angles
- Exhibitions change frequently, requiring rapid reconfiguration
- UV and IR radiation must be eliminated to protect artwork
- Lighting must render colors accurately (CRI > 95)

## Solution

We installed a DMX512-controlled track lighting system:

- **Interchangeable lens modules** allowing beam angle adjustment from 8° to 60°
- **Tunable white LEDs** (2700K–5000K) with CRI 97+
- **Scene programming software** for one-click reconfiguration between exhibitions
- **Wireless DMX** for flexible fixture placement without additional wiring

## Results

- **Exhibition changeover time** reduced from 3 days to 4 hours
- **Energy savings** of 55% compared to the previous halogen system
- **Zero UV/IR exposure** protecting invaluable artwork
- Curators can now program lighting scenes independently without technical support
